Write 3 call-to-action sections for "Glamour Studio", a salon in 
Banjara Hills, Hyderabad.

CTA 1 - Booking prompt: Encourage visitors to book an appointment. 
Include a sense of ease ("it takes 30 seconds").

CTA 2 - Trust builder: Mention experience, number of happy clients, 
or Google reviews. Make it feel earned, not boastful.

CTA 3 - Location/urgency CTA: Use the Banjara Hills location as a 
selling point. Add mild urgency ("limited weekend slots").

For each CTA, write:
- A short heading (5-8 words)
- Supporting text (1-2 sentences)
- Button text (2-4 words)

Tone: Confident, warm, action-oriented.
